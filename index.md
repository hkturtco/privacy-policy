Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to **JLPT 文法 (JLPT Flashcards)**, including its Android app and web app (the “App”). It explains what information is handled when you use the App.

## Information handled by the App

The developer does not ask you to create an account and does not operate a server that collects your personal information. The App does not send your flashcard activity or settings to the developer.

The App stores preferences and study information—such as your selected language and colors, saved or learned cards, and practice progress—locally on your device using browser or app storage. This information stays on your device and is not available to the developer. You can remove it by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the Android app. Clearing browser site data removes information stored by the web version.

## Advertising and Google

The Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to request and display ads. Google may automatically collect and process information such as your IP address, advertising or other device identifiers, app interactions (for example, launches and ad views), and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for purposes that may include delivering and measuring ads, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google may share information with its advertising partners as described in Google's policies.

The developer does not receive or store this information through the App. Google's handling of information is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your device and Google account settings. The web version does not currently include an advertising SDK.

## Children

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because Google ads are used in the Android app, the developer must configure ad requests and the store listing appropriately if the App is directed to children or is used by an audience that includes children.

## Data retention and security

Study information remains in local device storage until you clear it or uninstall the Android app. The developer does not keep a copy. Ad-related information may be retained by Google under its own policies. The App does not control Google's retention or security practices.

## Your choices

You may stop using the App at any time. You can clear locally stored App data in your device or browser settings. For information or choices related to Google's data practices, use the links above and the privacy controls provided by Google and your device.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use.
